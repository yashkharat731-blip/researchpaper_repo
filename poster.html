📌 Problem & Motivation

OVERVIEW

With over 5 million research papers indexed across arXiv and IEEE, researchers suffer from acute information overload. Traditional keyword search engines (lexical BM25 / TF-IDF) fail on research queries due to:

  • ✕
    Vocabulary Mismatch: Inability to map synonymous terms (e.g. "quantization" vs "weight compression").
  • ✕
    Absence of Explainability: Classical search tools provide no justification of why a paper was retrieved.
  • ✕
    Disconnected Review Workflow: Users must switch between search, PDF readers, note-taking, and citation managers.

🎯 System Objectives

GOALS
  • ✓
    Semantic Literature Discovery: Retrieve top relevant papers from live arXiv and curated datasets based on semantic topic vectors.
  • ✓
    LLM-Powered Explainability: Synthesize concise 1-sentence explanations clarifying exactly why each paper aligns with the research intent.
  • ✓
    In-Depth Paper Critique Agent: Dissect uploaded PDFs into Executive Summaries, Methodologies, and Strengths/Weaknesses.
  • ✓
    Conversational RAG Engine: Interactive multi-turn chat over paper content with verified page-level citations.

📐 Mathematical Foundations

ALGORITHMS

Each candidate paper abstract $d_i$ and the user's research query $q$ are projected into a dense vector space $\mathbb{R}^{384}$ using the transformer encoder:

1. Dense Embedding Vectorization
\mathbf{v}_q = f_\theta(\text{query}), \quad \mathbf{v}_{d_i} = f_\theta(\text{abstract}_i)

Both vectors are $L_2$-normalized such that $\|\mathbf{v}\|_2 = 1.0$. The semantic relevance is calculated using Cosine Similarity via FAISS Inner Product:

2. Cosine Similarity via Normalized Dot Product
\text{Sim}(\mathbf{q}, \mathbf{d}_i) = \frac{\mathbf{v}_q \cdot \mathbf{v}_{d_i}}{\|\mathbf{v}_q\|_2 \|\mathbf{v}_{d_i}\|_2} = \sum_{k=1}^{384} \hat{q}_k \hat{d}_{i,k}

Candidate ranking is performed by top-$k$ nearest neighbors:

3. Top-K Neighbor Selection
\mathcal{R}^* = \operatorname{arg\,top-}k_{i} \; \text{Sim}(\mathbf{q}, \mathbf{d}_i)

💡 Architectural Highlights

Decoupled Vectorization:

Dense embeddings execute entirely locally on CPU via SentenceTransformers, eliminating latency and API costs for standard ranking queries.

Generative Explanations:

Google Gemini 1.5 Flash acts as a zero-shot critic to deliver contextual justifications without hallucination through low-temperature prompting ($T=0.2$).

🧩 Modular Implementation

CODEBASE
1. PaperRecommender Engine recommender.py

Connects to arXiv API, calculates 384-d vector embeddings, constructs in-memory FAISS indices, and coordinates similarity scoring.

2. Deep Paper Critic paper_analyzer.py

PyMuPDF stream parser & Gemini agent performing rigorous 5-pillar academic evaluations (methodology, limitations, novelty).

3. Conversational RAG Engine tools.py

Document chunker (1000/200 tokens) with FAISS vector store backing multi-turn conversational document Q&A.

4. Interactive Dashboard app.py

Streamlit application featuring glassmorphism design, Plotly similarity visualizers, and instant BibTeX/APA citation generators.
